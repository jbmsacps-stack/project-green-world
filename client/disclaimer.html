Website Information

What should visitors know?

Educational Purpose

Project Green World is created for educational and awareness purposes. The information is meant to help visitors understand global warming in a simple way.

No Professional Advice

The content on this website should not be treated as professional scientific, legal, financial, medical, or environmental advice.

External Links

Some pages may include external links for learning or donation purposes. Visitors should verify official websites before trusting or donating through any link.

Content Accuracy

We try to keep the information clear and useful, but climate information can change over time. Visitors should refer to trusted scientific and official sources for updated details.

Final Note

By using this website, visitors understand that Project Green World is an awareness project and not an official climate authority.
